For custom cakes, 2–3 days notice is recommended. Signature cakes may have faster availability.

Can I request a different flavor or color?

Yes. Use the custom cake form and describe your preferred flavor, colors and theme.

Do you deliver across Karachi?

We offer Karachi delivery. Delivery timing and charges can vary by area and order size.

How do I place an order?

Add cakes to your basket or use the custom order form. The website prepares your order details for WhatsApp.
